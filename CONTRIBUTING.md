# Contributing

Contributions welcome. **No One Is as Dumb as All of Us**, and volunteer organizations already share everything else at the district roundtable, this belongs there too.

## Useful

- The scam your club actually saw, scrubbed to the pattern. The repertoire page should grow the way scams do.
- Handover checklists that survived a real June, and what yours added.
- State-specific wrinkles, clearly labeled: raffle rules, association audit checklists, registration quirks.
- Simplifications. The readers here are volunteers with day jobs, every shortened sentence is a service.

## Not Useful

- Anything club-identifiable, school-identifiable, or about specific kids or families. Ever. This is the brightest line on the account.
- Vendor and platform promotion. Categories over brands, and the free-controls promise is the repo's spine.
- Enterprise depth. The treasurer with a day job is the reader; content that needs an IT department belongs in the other repos.

## How

Open an issue first for anything big. Small fixes straight to a PR.

## Style

Plain English, warm, written for a board meeting handout. Say why, not just what. Short. The index card is the format ideal, and dignity is the tone: every control here protects the volunteers, and the writing should sound like it knows that.
