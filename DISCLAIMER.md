**Your School District's Rules Win.** Where a district or school has policies on parent organizations, data, photos, or funds, theirs outrank these pages everywhere they overlap.

**About Kids, Extra Care.** The data and photo guidance here is a floor, not a ceiling. Individual family circumstances, custody situations, safety concerns, can make the standard practice wrong for a specific child, and the family's word governs, always, without explanation required.

**Every Club Is Different.** A three-parent robotics club and a 400-family band program need different amounts of this. Adapt, and keep what your size can actually sustain, a sustained simple control beats an abandoned thorough one.
