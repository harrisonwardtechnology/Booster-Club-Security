# The Treasurer's Page

> [!NOTE]
> Draft. These controls protect the money, and they protect the treasurer more. Adopt them for yourself, not against anyone.

**Purpose.** The money rules for a volunteer organization, all free, all boring, all battle-tested.

## Two People on Every Payment

No single person moves club money alone, ever: two authorized signers on checks, dual approval on electronic payments where the bank supports it, and where a platform cannot enforce it, a written rule that every payment gets a second officer's yes in writing first. This is not about suspicion. A two-person rule means no volunteer can ever be accused alone, blamed alone, or tempted alone, and the treasurer who insists on it is protecting their own name hardest of all. It also happens to defeat most fraud, because the [fake invoice](scams-that-target-clubs.md) has to fool two people on two channels instead of one tired parent at 10pm.

## Verify Before You Pay, Club Edition

Any new payee, changed bank details, or urgent request, including from the president, gets a phone call to a number you already had before acting. The [full protocol](https://github.com/HarrisonWard/Verify-Before-You-Pay) is a sibling repo; the club version fits on an index card taped inside the treasurer binder: known number, not the one in the email. Urgent plus secret equals scam. Nobody is ever in trouble for checking. Read the [scams page](scams-that-target-clubs.md) for why this one habit is most of the defense.

## The Club's Money Lives in the Club's Accounts

A real bank account in the organization's name, never a personal account holding club funds even briefly, and payment apps, where the club uses them, set up as business or organization accounts owned by the club, not a volunteer's personal Venmo doing double duty. Mixing funds is how honest people end up in dishonest-looking positions, and untangling it at handover time is misery. Turn on the bank's transaction alerts to two officers' phones, the free fraud detection nobody uses.

## Sunshine Is the Control

Monthly, at the board meeting: the reconciled numbers, and at least quarterly, someone who is not the treasurer looks at the actual bank statements against the books. Annually, a simple review by two members outside the signer group, most state PTA and booster associations publish exactly this audit checklist, use theirs. None of this is dramatic, and that is the point: consistent small sunshine prevents the quiet multi-year losses that make the news, and it means the books' health never rests on one person's word, which is a gift to that person.

## The Index Card Summary

Two signatures always. Callback before any new or changed payment. Club money only in club accounts. Alerts on. Numbers shown monthly, statements seen quarterly, reviewed annually. Tape it in the binder, hand it to your successor in [June](accounts-and-handover.md).
